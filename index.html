About Velora Counselling

Velora Counselling is inspired by the combination of “Well” and “Aura,” symbolising inner wellness and balanced positive energy. We believe every person has the capacity to heal, grow, and thrive when given the right support.

Vision

To create a world where mental wellness is accessible, stigma-free, and embraced as an essential part of overall wellbeing.

Mission

  • Provide a safe, confidential, and non-judgmental space.
  • Support emotional balance, personal growth, and self-awareness.
  • Promote mental health awareness and reduce stigma.
  • Deliver professional, ethical, and culturally sensitive counselling services.
  • Empower individuals to cultivate wellbeing and positive life direction.

Payment Policy

  • Full payment is required at least 24 hours before the scheduled session.
  • Payment details will be provided upon booking confirmation.
  • Sessions are only confirmed once payment is received.
  • Package sessions require full payment upfront unless otherwise agreed.

Cancellation & Rescheduling

  • Rescheduling is free if done at least 24 hours in advance.
  • Late cancellations and no-shows are fully charged.
  • Emergency exceptions may be considered case by case.
  • Late arrivals still end at the scheduled time.
